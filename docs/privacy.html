Privacy Policy

Media · Last updated: October 2026

Media is a local media player for Android. It is built to respect your privacy by design. This policy explains, plainly, what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

Media itself does not collect, sell, or share personal data. It has no accounts and no analytics, and your media, edits and history never leave your device. The free version shows one banner ad in Settings, supplied by Google AdMob; Google's ad software may collect data such as your device's advertising identifier to serve it, as described under Advertising below.

Information the app accesses

To function as a media player, Media reads media files already stored on your device:

  • Audio and video files — so it can list and play your music, podcasts, audiobooks, and videos. This uses Android's media permissions, which you grant on first launch.
  • Cover artwork embedded in those files, shown in the app.

This information is read only to display and play your media. It is never uploaded or sent anywhere.

Information the app stores

Media stores a small amount of data locally on your device, never off it:

  • Your edits — custom titles, artist/author names, and category choices you make for your files.
  • Play history — which items you played recently, to power the "Continue" row.
  • Preferences — your theme and text-size choices.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app removes it. None of it leaves your device.

Optional online artwork and lyrics

Media can look up missing cover art and lyrics. This is off until you turn it on in Settings, or ask for a cover or lyrics for a single track. When it runs, only that track's title, artist, album and length are sent: for covers to MusicBrainz, Cover Art Archive, Deezer and Apple's iTunes Search; for lyrics to LRCLIB, NetEase Cloud Music and lyrics.ovh. The covers and lyrics that come back are stored in the app's private storage. Nothing is written into your files, and no identifier for you or your device is sent.

If you choose a lyrics folder in Settings, Media reads the names and contents of the lyrics files in that folder only, on your device. If you add lyrics yourself, from a file or by pasting, they are kept in the app's private storage.

Advertising

The free version shows a single banner ad in the Settings screen, supplied by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To serve and measure that ad, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and your interactions with the ad. In the UK, the EEA and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested; if you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time in Android's settings. Google's use of this data is governed by its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Ads can be removed with a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google processes the payment; Media only learns whether the purchase is owned, and keeps that on your device.

What the app does not do

  • No internet connection is required to play your media, and Media itself sends nothing about you anywhere.
  • No personal information is sold.
  • No analytics or tracking libraries are included. The only third-party SDKs are Google Mobile Ads with Google's consent SDK, and Google Play Billing.
  • No account or sign-in is required.

Children's privacy

Media is not directed at children. The app itself collects no information from anyone; the ad SDK described above works under Google's policies.
